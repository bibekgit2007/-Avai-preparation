🩺 NEET (UG) National Eligibility Cum Entrance Test

🖥️ Official CBT Mode Mandated (2026/2027)
  • Exam Delivery Mode (Official CBT Transition): NEET (UG) is officially moving to an online Computer-Based Test (CBT) / Multi-Stage Digital Mode as mandated by the Ministry of Education (MoE) High-Level Committee on Exam Reforms (chaired by former ISRO Chairman Dr. K. Radhakrishnan) for NEET 2026/2027 onwards, replacing the vulnerable paper OMR format. Practicing on this AVAI computer-based interface directly equips aspirants for the official digital CBT exam!
  • Total Duration: 3 Hours 20 Minutes (200 minutes) from 02:00 PM to 05:20 PM.
  • Pattern: 200 MCQs (attempt 180). Section A (35 questions, mandatory) + Section B (15 questions, attempt any 10).
  • Marking Scheme: +4 marks for correct response; -1 mark for wrong response; 0 for unattempted.
  • Dress Code: Light clothes with half sleeves (no long sleeves or big buttons allowed). Slippers or sandals with low heels; shoes are strictly prohibited.
  • Barred Items: Calculators, electronic watches, mobile phones, geometry boxes, and metallic items are strictly banned in the exam hall.
  • Mandatory Documents: Printed Admit Card with passport photo affixed, Original valid Govt Photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport), One postcard-size photo on proforma, and One passport-size photo for attendance sheet.

⚡ JEE Main (B.E. / B.Tech)

Computer Based Test (CBT)
  • Total Duration: 3 Hours (180 minutes).
  • Pattern: 75 questions total (25 Physics, 25 Chemistry, 25 Mathematics). Section A has 20 MCQs; Section B has 5 Numerical Value Questions (all compulsory, no optional choices).
  • Marking Scheme: +4 marks for correct response; -1 mark for incorrect response (applied to both Section A and Section B Numerical Questions).
  • Rough Work: 6 blank rough sheets provided inside the exam lab; must write Roll Number and Name at the top and drop in the drop-box before leaving.
  • Virtual Tools: No on-screen calculator provided in JEE Main. (Virtual scientific calculator is permitted only in GATE & BITSAT).
