Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data Storage & Privacy

AVAI Preparation respects your privacy. All mock test responses, chosen options, timer states, and calculator history are stored entirely locally on your device via your browser's Local Storage. We do not collect or transmit your personal identifiable test data to external third parties.

2. Progressive Web App & Offline Caching

This application utilizes standard Service Workers and Progressive Web App (PWA) caching mechanisms to allow offline exam practice and fast load times.

3. Third-Party CDN Resources

Our portal uses high-reliability Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) including Google Fonts, Tailwind CSS, and PapaParse for script delivery.

4. Contact

For inquiries, feedback, or suggestions regarding AVAI Preparation, feel free to reach out to the developer and educator team.
